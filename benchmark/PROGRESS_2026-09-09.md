# Conversion fidelity progress — September 9, 2026

The ten-app functional/usability goal is still open. Eleven pinned public
exports can be restored: the five `samples/real` apps and six Microsoft business
exports. All generate valid code. The five regression apps and Microsoft
Employee Ideas pass the short startup check. The other five Microsoft apps
fail startup, with saved views now exposing previously silent missing identity
mappings and unsupported relative-date filters. All three default first-action
probes fail. Populated Employee Ideas passes 29 checks through mobile validation,
custom text questions, submission, persistence, reload, reopening and campaign
idea counts. Its optional voting probe persists a count of one and executes
relationship operations, but fails membership: the exported Concurrent runs an
unconditional Unrelate that removes the voter link in this run.
Ratings, attachments, manager workflows and
complete app usability remain unassessed.
No fixture or clean loading screen
is counted as a usable real app.
